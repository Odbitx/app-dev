# My Favorite Movie

## 🎬 Good Will Hunting

**Good Will Hunting** is my favorite movie because it tells a powerful story about *self-discovery*, intelligence, and emotional growth.  
It’s about a young janitor at MIT who secretly has an extraordinary gift for mathematics.

### Main Reasons I Like It
1. **Strong Characters** — The acting and dialogue feel real and honest.  
2. **Deep Themes** — It explores identity, trauma, and potential in a meaningful way.  
3. **Emotional Moments** — The bond between Will and his therapist is unforgettable.  

### Favorite Quote
> "You’ll have bad times, but it’ll always wake you up to the good stuff you weren’t paying attention to."

### Cast Highlights
- Matt Damon as *Will Hunting*  
- Robin Williams as *Sean Maguire*  
- Ben Affleck as *Chuckie Sullivan*
